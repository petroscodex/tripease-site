## Points to confirm before shipping
1. **App Store and Google Play listings:** the terms do not mention app stores specifically. Nothing found forbids them, but this has not been confirmed in writing. If you want certainty, ask xAI support.
2. **Copyright:** owning an AI output under the provider's terms is not the same as holding copyright in it. In several countries purely AI-generated images may not be copyrightable, so others could copy them without breaching our copyright.
3. **Plan and terms may change.** Re-check the terms if a later release depends on them. Keep a copy of the Terms page as of the generation date.
4. **Which service made these files:** confirm that the account used is the same Grok Imagine service covered by the terms above before the credit line goes live.

No readable text, flags, logos, liveries, real landmarks or recognisable faces. Every image was checked by eye at the preview size. Zoom in at full size on tails, luggage and signs before release.
