TripEase

Short description

TripEase helps you arrive prepared: entry rules for your passport, links to the official arrival forms, and help for your first hour on the ground. No account; your passport stays on your phone.

Long description

TripEase is a calm travel companion for iPhone, iPad and Android. It shows the entry rules for your passport across 250 countries and territories, links straight to official government visa and arrival-form sites, and offers official terminal maps for 177 airports. Trips get a countdown and a smart checklist. A copy-only passport profile, an offline wallet and a stamp map of everywhere you've been round it out. Available in 9 languages. Free to start; TripEase Pro adds more passports, unlimited trips and more.
