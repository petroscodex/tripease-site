🇯🇵 Arrival guide · Japan

Arrive in Japan prepared.

The official links you need, the way into town, and the small things that make the first hour easy. Rules change: we always point you to the government's own site.

Before you fly

What do I need to enter Japan?

It depends on your passport. Check the official source for your nationality: Ministry of Foreign Affairs of Japan: visa information ↗. TripEase shows the rule for your exact passport and links straight to it.

Optional

Visit Japan Web

Optional for visitors. When: Before you travel. See the official site for timing. Cost: Free on the official site.

Open the official site ↗ Our guide to it

Run by Digital Agency of Japan.

Only use the official site. Lookalike sites copy official forms and sometimes charge for them. Check the address before you type anything.

Just landed

From the airport into town

Each airport publishes its own trains, buses and taxi options. Start there, not on a forum:

  • Narita International (NRT): official airport site ↗
  • Tokyo Haneda (HND): official airport site ↗
  • Kansai International (KIX): official airport site ↗

Take taxis from the official rank or use a ride app you trust. Save your accommodation address offline before you fly; TripEase's driver card shows it in the local language.

Data & money

eSIM, money and plugs

eSIM

If your phone is unlocked and supports eSIM, install a travel eSIM on Wi-Fi before you fly and turn it on when you land. Compare it with your own carrier's roaming price.

Money

The currency is the Japanese yen (JPY). Tell your bank you're travelling, carry a card that works abroad, and keep a little cash for your first ride.

Plugs

Plug types A, B. Source: IEC World Plugs.

More for visitors: Japan National Tourism Organization ↗

Questions

Good to know

What do I need to enter Japan?

It depends on the passport you travel on. Check the official source (Ministry of Foreign Affairs of Japan: visa information: https://www.mofa.go.jp/j_info/visit/visa/index.html) for your nationality. Visit Japan Web: Optional for visitors. Submit: Before you travel. See the official site for timing. Official site: https://vjw-lp.digital.go.jp/en/ TripEase shows the rule for your passport and links straight to the official sites. It never fills in or submits forms for you.

Is the Visit Japan Web free?

Free on the official site. Only use the official site, https://vjw-lp.digital.go.jp/en/; lookalike sites can charge for it.

How do I get from the airport into the city in Japan?

Use the transport page on the airport's official website for trains, buses and taxis, and use official taxi ranks or ride apps you trust. TripEase's "I'm landed" screen puts these in one place.

What currency and plugs does Japan use?

The currency is the Japanese yen (JPY). Plug types: A, B (source: IEC World Plugs). Bring a little local money or a card that works abroad for your first hour.

Can I use an eSIM in Japan?

Usually yes, if your phone supports eSIM and is unlocked. Install it before you fly while you're on Wi-Fi, and switch it on when you land. Check your carrier's roaming prices too.
