Arrive in Canada prepared.

The official links you need, the way into town, and the small things that make the first hour easy. Rules change: we always point you to the government's own site.

Data & money

eSIM, money and plugs

eSIM

If your phone is unlocked and supports eSIM, install a travel eSIM on Wi-Fi before you fly and turn it on when you land. Compare it with your own carrier's roaming price.

Money

The currency is the Canadian dollar (CAD). Tell your bank you're travelling, carry a card that works abroad, and keep a little cash for your first ride.

Plugs

Plug types A, B. Source: IEC World Plugs.

Questions

Good to know

What do I need to enter Canada?

It depends on the passport you travel on. Check the official source (IRCC: visit Canada: https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/visit-canada.html) for your nationality. Electronic Travel Authorization (eTA): Visitors whose passport is covered by the scheme. Check your passport first: TripEase tells you whether you need it. Submit: Before you travel. See the official site for timing. Official site: https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/visit-canada/eta/apply.html TripEase shows the rule for your passport and links straight to the official sites. It never fills in or submits forms for you.

Is the Electronic Travel Authorization (eTA) free?

An official fee is charged on the official site. Lookalike sites add their own charges on top. Only use the official site, https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/visit-canada/eta/apply.html; lookalike sites can charge for it.

How do I get from the airport into the city in Canada?

Use the transport page on the airport's official website for trains, buses and taxis, and use official taxi ranks or ride apps you trust. TripEase's "I'm landed" screen puts these in one place.

What currency and plugs does Canada use?

The currency is the Canadian dollar (CAD). Plug types: A, B (source: IEC World Plugs). Bring a little local money or a card that works abroad for your first hour.

Can I use an eSIM in Canada?

Usually yes, if your phone supports eSIM and is unlocked. Install it before you fly while you're on Wi-Fi, and switch it on when you land. Check your carrier's roaming prices too.
