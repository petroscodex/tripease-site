# TripEase Korean glossary

**Status: pending native review.** Not reviewed by a native reader. These are the working choices behind `lib/l10n/app_ko.arb` (app) and `i18n/ko.json` (website).

Style: UI text and marketing in 해요체 (~해요/~이에요), used consistently; legal, privacy and credit text stays in 합쇼체 (~합니다). No stiff "당신의"; particles follow the preceding word (Land & Sea는, SGAC가). Brands and official form names stay in Latin letters (TripEase, Petros Codex, ETA, eTA, ESTA, TDAC, eSIM, Visit Japan Web).

| English | Korean | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Land & Sea (mode) | 육로·해상 | One name in app and site (was mixed). |
| Air (mode) | 항공 | Parallel to 육로·해상. |
| Entry requirements | 입국 요건 | What a traveller needs to enter. |
| Entry rules | 입국 규정 | Used only where rules (not requirements) are meant. |
| Arrival form / arrival card | 입국 신고서 | The everyday Korean name; replaces 입국 카드 / 입국 서류 in site text. |
| Form (official) | 양식 | Distinct from 서류 (documents). |
| Documents | 서류 | Only for documents, not forms. |
| Phone | 휴대폰 | Replaces mixed 스마트폰/핸드폰. |
| Free months (referral) | 무료 이용 기간 | Replaces literal "무료 월". |
| Crossing (border) | 검문소 | Kept for crossing points. |
| By road / ferry / train | 도로로도 / 페리로도 / 기차로도 건널 수 있어요 | Natural phrasing for "can be crossed by". |
