# TripEase Italian glossary (it)

**Status: pending native review.** Not natively reviewed. These are the working choices behind `lib/l10n/app_it.arb` (app) and `i18n/it.json` (website). Everything here is a proposal for a native reader to confirm or change.

Style rules used throughout: informal "tu" everywhere (imperatives in the tu form: "Scopri…", "Controlla…"); calm, plain register; curly apostrophes (’) and curly quotes (“ ”) in the app and the site; "checklist" (not "lista di controllo") everywhere; "app" stays "app"; brands stay "TripEase" and "Petros Codex"; official form names and acronyms stay Latin (ETA, eTA, ESTA, TDAC, eSIM, eVisa); official agency names stay in their original language on the website; prices on the website read "29,99 US$".

| English | Italian | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Land & Sea (the mode and page) | Terra e mare | Natural Italian for the feature name; lower-case "mare" like normal Italian titles. The app used "Terra e Mare" (capital M) in some places. Same wording in app and site. **Check with a native reader.** |
| Air (the mode tab) | Volo | The app had "Aria", which reads as the element (air). "Volo" parallels the "Vola" start card. **Check with a native reader** (alternative: "Aereo"). |
| Cruise (mode) | Crociera | Unchanged. |
| Rides (ride-hailing apps) | Taxi e auto | "Passaggi" means lifts/hitch-hiking in Italian. |
| Partner picks | Consigli dei partner | Replaces "scelte dei partner", which read like a literal translation. |
| Travel advisory | Avviso di viaggio | Unchanged. |
| Customs (the border authority) | Dogana / regole doganali | "Le usanze variano" was wrong: "usanze" means habits. Fixed to "Le regole doganali variano". |
| Driver card | Scheda autista (“Scheda da mostrare all’autista” in help text) | The card is shown to the driver, so the sentence on it now uses the polite form ("Mi porti a questo indirizzo…, per favore"), so the driver hears a polite request. |
| I’ve landed | Sono atterrato / Appena atterrato | Unchanged. Hub card is "Sono arrivato". |
| Wallet (offline) | Portafoglio di viaggio | Unchanged. |
| Checklist | Checklist | The app mixed "checklist" and "lista di controllo"; now always "checklist". |
| Visa-free | Senza visto | Unchanged. |
| Travel authorisation | Autorizzazione di viaggio | Used for ETA/eTA/ESTA/K-ETA; the acronym stays. |
| Open (button/fallback label) | Apri | "Aperto" read like a status adjective. |
| Form helper | Assistente moduli | Unchanged. |
| Stamp map | Mappa dei timbri | Unchanged. |

## Website-specific choices
| English | Italian | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Help centre | Aiuto / Centro assistenza | Menu label "Aiuto", "Aiuto e note legali" for the footer. |
| Clear / Check first / Stop and plan (status tags) | Via libera / Richiedi prima / Fermati e pianifica | Three-step status in country guides. **Check with a native reader.** |
| Arrival guide: {country} | Guida all’arrivo: {country} | Breadcrumbs and page titles. |
| Official agency names (e.g. Immigration Department of Malaysia) | kept in the original language | Names of foreign government bodies are not translated, to avoid inventing names. |
| Prices | 29,99 US$ / 4,99 US$ | Comma decimal and "US$" suffix (the English "$29.99" is read as US dollars). |
| Dates (“Last checked”) | 20/10/2026 | Day/month/year. |
| Legal pages (privacy, terms) | stay English | Per the brief. |
