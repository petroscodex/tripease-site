# TripEase Spanish glossary (es)

**Status: pending native review.** Not natively reviewed. These are the working choices behind `lib/l10n/app_es.arb` (app) and `i18n/es.json` (website). Everything here is a proposal for a native reader to confirm or change.

Style rules used throughout: informal "tú" everywhere (imperatives in the tú form: "Entérate de…", never the third-person "Sabe…"); calm, plain register; neutral Spanish that works in Spain and Latin America where possible; "aviso" for caution/notice texts, never the stronger "advertencia"; currency adjectives lower-case ("el yen japonés"); prices as "29,99 US$"; percentages with a non-breaking-style space ("50 %"); curly quotation marks or «guillemets» for quoted UI labels; brands stay "TripEase" and "Petros Codex"; official form names and acronyms stay Latin (ETA, eTA, ESTA, TDAC, eSIM, eVisa).

| English | Spanish | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Land & Sea (the mode and page) | Tierra y mar | Natural Spanish for the feature name; the site previously kept the English "Land & Sea". Now identical in app and site. **Check with a native reader.** |
| Air (the mode) | Avión | Short tab label next to "Tierra y mar"; "Aire" would read as the element. |
| Partner (affiliate partner) | Colaborador | "Socio" suggests business ownership or membership; "colaborador" is what a traveller expects for a flagged recommendation. |
| Trip | Viaje | Plain. |
| Travel advisory / caution | Aviso de viaje | Matches the softer meaning of the source; "advertencia" is too strong. |
| Duty-free shopping | Compras libres de impuestos | Replaces the literal "compras sin impuestos". |
| Driver card | Tarjeta del conductor | The card with the hotel address in the local language. |
| I've landed | He llegado | Used for the "I've landed" screen in app and site; "Recién llegado" for the section label. |
| Wallet (offline) | Cartera | Used in app and site (was "monedero" on the site). |
| Phone | Móvil / teléfono | The site says "móvil", the app mostly "teléfono": a native reader should decide whether to align (Latin American readers may prefer "celular"). |
| Entry requirements | Requisitos de entrada | Plain. |
| Visa-free | Sin visado | Standard in Spain; "sin visa" is the Latin American form. |
| e-Visa | eVisa | As used by the app. |
| Travel authorisation | Autorización de viaje | Used for ETA/eTA/ESTA/K-ETA; the acronym stays. |
