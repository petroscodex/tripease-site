# TripEase French glossary (fr)

**Status: pending native review.** Not natively reviewed. These are the working choices behind `lib/l10n/app_fr.arb` (app) and `i18n/fr.json` (website). Everything here is a proposal for a native reader to confirm or change.

Style rules used throughout: formal "vous" everywhere (four leftover "tu" strings in the app were fixed); calm, plain register; typographic apostrophe ’ in the app (the website keeps its straight ' as before); « guillemets » with spaces for quoted UI labels; "appli" (never a mix of "app" and "appli"); prices as "29,99 US$"; brands stay "TripEase" and "Petros Codex"; official form names and acronyms stay Latin (ETA, eTA, ESTA, TDAC, eSIM, eVisa).

| English | French | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Land & Sea (the mode and page) | Terre et mer | Already used in the app; the website kept the English "Land & Sea" in ~20 places. Now identical in app and site. **Check with a native reader.** |
| Air / Fly (the mode) | Avion (tab) / En avion (start card) | "Air" alone is an odd mode label; "Voler" on the site also means "to steal", so it is now "En avion". |
| Cruise (mode) | Croisière | Unchanged. |
| Partner picks | Recommandations partenaires | "Choix partenaires" was literal. |
| Disclaimer | Avis de non-responsabilité | "Avertissement" alone reads like a warning. |
| Landed / I've arrived | Je suis arrivé | Used in app and site (site said "J'ai atterri"). |
| Welcome ({country}) | Bienvenue : {country} | Keeps the placeholder grammatical for every country. |
| App | Appli | The app mixed "app" and "appli"; now "appli/applis" everywhere ("App Store" is unchanged). |
| Wallet | Portefeuille (de voyage) | Unchanged. |
| Phone | Téléphone | Used in app and site. |
| Entry requirements | Conditions d’entrée (app) / Règles d'entrée (site) | Both were already in use; "règles" is the shorter form used in titles. A native reader may want to align. |
| Visa-free | Sans visa | Standard. |
| Travel authorisation | Autorisation de voyage | Used for ETA/eTA/ESTA/K-ETA; the acronym stays. |
| Caution / warnings | Avis | "Mises en garde" was heavier than the source. |
