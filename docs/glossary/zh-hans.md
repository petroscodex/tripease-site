# TripEase Simplified Chinese (zh-Hans) glossary

**Status: pending native review.** Not reviewed by a native reader. Working choices behind `lib/l10n/app_zh.arb` (app) and `i18n/zh-hans.json` (website).

Style: informal-polite 你 throughout (no 您); 点按 for "tap" (not 轻点/点击); 朋友 for friends; no stray spaces after 。; brands and official form names stay in Latin letters (TripEase, Petros Codex, ETA, eTA, ESTA, TDAC, eSIM, Visit Japan Web). The website is China-facing and contains no Taiwan terms.

| English | Simplified Chinese | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Land & Sea (mode) | 陆路与海路 | The app already used it; the website now matches instead of leaving "Land & Sea" in English. |
| Air (mode) | 航空 | Parallel to 陆路与海路 / 邮轮. |
| Arrival form | 入境表格 | Used app- and site-wide. |
| Arrival card (official name, e.g. TDAC) | 入境卡 | Follows the English source term for official "arrival cards". |
| Visa-free | 免签 | App had 免签证 in one string; unified to 免签. |
| Tap | 点按 | Apple/Android term; replaces 轻点. |
| Friends / invite | 朋友 / 邀请朋友 | Site had 好友. |
| This phone | 这部手机 | Unchanged. |
| Qibla | 麦加方向 | Plain Chinese instead of the unexplained loan word; the title keeps (Qibla). |
| Move to a new phone | 换到新手机 | Strings were still English in the app. |
