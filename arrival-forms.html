Arrival forms

The official forms, and only the official forms.

Arrival cards and travel authorisations, with the real government link, when to submit and who needs it. Most arrival cards are free. If a site asks you to pay for one, it isn't the official site.

    Canada · Required for covered passports

    India · Required
  • 🇮🇩All Indonesia arrival card
    Indonesia · Required
  • 🇯🇵Visit Japan Web
    Japan · Optional
