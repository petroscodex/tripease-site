Questions

Good to know before you download

Visit the help centre

Cruises and trip tracking are coming as optional, opt-in extras as we build them.

Is Tripveller only for flights?

No. Tripveller helps with flights and, through Land & Sea, land, ferry and sea crossings. Land & Sea is free in the main app: opening hours, notes and the official links for each crossing. Rules and hours can change, so always check with the officials before you travel. Cruises and trip tracking are coming as optional, opt-in extras as we build them.

Is the visa information up to date?

Entry rules are refreshed every week from an open dataset and official government sources. Each country shows when it was last checked and links to the official site, because rules can change at short notice.

Does Tripveller submit forms for me?

No, and that's on purpose. The form helper shows your details in the order the official form asks for them, with copy buttons. You always submit on the government's own site.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-up. Your passport, trips and documents are stored only on your phone.

What's free and what's in Pro?

Free gives you entry rules and official links for any passport, Land & Sea (land, ferry and sea crossings), the stamp map, 1 saved passport and 2 trips in any 12 months. Pro (US$4.99 a month or US$29.99 a year, with a 14-day free trial) adds up to 10 travellers with up to 3 passports each and the best one picked per country, unlimited trips, the copy-only form helper, the offline wallet, the Home Screen widget and Lock Screen countdown, reminders for optional forms, the Schengen 90/180 counter, every local service per country, landing weather, and Family Sharing on the yearly plan.

What is Land & Sea?

Land & Sea helps you cross borders by road, rail or ferry. It lists 1,022 crossings in 134 countries and territories with official opening hours where they are published, cautions where hours can change, and links to the official entry, customs and crossing pages. It is free, part of the main app.

Does Tripveller cover plugs, tipping and local apps?

Yes. Each destination shows its plug types and voltage, a short tipping note taken from an official tourism or government page, and the ride, transit and SIM apps that operate there. Plug pictures, the tipping guide and the local-apps overview are on this site too.

What happens if Pro ends?

If Pro ends, you keep the free app. Anything you made with Pro stays viewable but is locked until you resubscribe, and nothing is deleted.

What else is in the app?

Details for 1,182 airports with official terminal maps for 184, a stamp map for each passport, a free emergency card and driver card, and ride, transit and SIM apps for 186 countries and territories: 5 per country on Free, all of them on Pro. Pro can also import trips from your booking confirmations.

Which languages does it speak?

Nineteen: English, Chinese (Simplified and Traditional), Japanese, Korean, Thai, German, French, Spanish, Arabic, Portuguese, Hindi, Indonesian, Russian, Turkish, Italian, Vietnamese, Bengali, Urdu and Filipino.

How does Tripveller make money?

From Pro subscriptions and labelled partner picks. We never sell your data, and official visa links are never partner links.
