Your details, ready to copy

The form helper lines up your details in the order the official form asks for them, with copy buttons. You submit on the official site yourself.

  • eSIM, money with a currency converter, and ride apps that work there
  • Emergency numbers that work offline

Private by design

Your passport never leaves your phone.

A travel app shouldn't need your identity on a server. No accounts, no server copy of your details. Nothing to leak, nothing to worry about.

On-device scanning

Your passport is read on the phone. No photo is taken or kept.

Offline wallet

Your trip details and documents stay on your phone, ready without signal.

No accounts

No sign-up, no profile on a server. Delete the app and it's gone.

Official links only

Visa and form links always go to government sites. Partner links are always labelled.

Questions

Good to know before you download

Visit the help centre

Sea and Land modes are coming later.

Is the visa information up to date?

Entry rules are refreshed every week from an open dataset and official government sources. Each country shows when it was last checked and links to the official site, because rules can change at short notice.

Does TripEase submit forms for me?

No, and that's on purpose. The form helper shows your details in the order the official form asks for them, with copy buttons. You always submit on the government's own site.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-up. Your passport, trips and documents are stored only on your phone.

What's free and what's in Pro?

Free gives you entry rules and official links for any passport, 2 trips in total and 1 saved passport. Pro (US$2.99 a month or US$19.99 a year, with a 14-day free trial) adds up to 6 passports, unlimited trips, Family Sharing on the yearly plan for up to 6 family members, the full stamp map, arrival preview links and landing weather.

Which languages does it speak?

Nine: English, Chinese (Simplified and Traditional), Japanese, Korean, Thai, German, French and Spanish.

How does TripEase make money?

From Pro subscriptions and labelled partner picks. We never sell your data, and official visa links are never partner links.
