Questions

Good to know before you download

Visit the help centre

Cruise Pass is coming later.

Is TripEase only for flights?

No. TripEase helps with flights, land crossings and ferries. Land crossings and ferries are free in the main app: opening hours, notes and the official links for each crossing. Rules and hours can change, so always check with the officials before you travel. Cruise Pass is coming later.

Is the visa information up to date?

Entry rules are refreshed every week from an open dataset and official government sources. Each country shows when it was last checked and links to the official site, because rules can change at short notice.

Does TripEase submit forms for me?

No, and that's on purpose. The form helper shows your details in the order the official form asks for them, with copy buttons. You always submit on the government's own site.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-up. Your passport, trips and documents are stored only on your phone.

What's free and what's in Pro?

Free gives you entry rules and official links for any passport, land crossings and ferries, the stamp map, 1 saved passport and 2 trips in any 12 months. Pro (US$4.99 a month or US$29.99 a year, with a 14-day free trial) adds up to 6 passports with the best one picked per country, unlimited trips, the copy-only form helper, the offline wallet, the Home Screen widget and Lock Screen countdown, reminders for optional forms, the Schengen 90/180 counter, every local service per country, landing weather, and Family Sharing on the yearly plan.

What is Land mode?

Land mode helps you cross borders by road, rail or ferry. It lists 468 crossings in 91 countries and territories with official opening hours where they are published, cautions where hours can change, and links to the official entry, customs and crossing pages. It is free, part of the main app.

Does TripEase cover plugs, tipping and local apps?

Yes. Each destination shows its plug types and voltage, a short tipping note taken from an official tourism or government page, and the ride, transit and SIM apps that operate there. Plug pictures, the tipping guide and the local-apps overview are on this site too.

Which languages does it speak?

Nine: English, Chinese (Simplified and Traditional), Japanese, Korean, Thai, German, French and Spanish.

How does TripEase make money?

From Pro subscriptions and labelled partner picks. We never sell your data, and official visa links are never partner links.
