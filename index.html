Where are you landing?

TripEase covers 250 countries and territories and 1,150 airports. It shows the entry rules for your passport, links straight to the official arrival forms and terminal maps, and keeps a checklist for every trip — calm, practical, and ready when you are.

On your phone

Real screens from the app, shown with a made-up traveller: your trips, the entry rules for your passport, a trip checklist with the official form link, your travel profile, and an airport guide.

What you get

Built for the moments before you fly and after you land — clear information, official links, and recommendations that feel like advice, not ads.

🛂

Entry rules for your passport

See whether you need a visa, an eVisa or a travel authorisation, for your own passport. Your passport details stay on your phone and are never uploaded.

📝

Official forms, linked

Buttons straight to the official arrival forms, eVisa portals and immigration sites, marked required or optional. TripEase never hosts or fills in a form. Always free.

🛫

Airport guides & maps

Guides for 1,150 airports, with a button that opens the airport’s official terminal map where it publishes one, and Google Maps alongside.

✓

A checklist per trip

Each trip builds its own checklist from that country’s rules, including every required form with its official link. It starts empty and stays on your phone.

🔒

Your passport stays on your phone

Scan the two lines at the bottom of your passport photo page, or type the details. The scan is read on your phone, nothing is uploaded and no photo is kept. Your profile and trips live only on your device.

🤝

Partner recommendations

Curated apps and services for eSIMs, stays, activities, money, and more — free to browse, labeled when they’re partners.

🔔

Travel updates

Advisory changes and upcoming public holidays for your destination, refreshed weekly. Live alerts are coming soon to TripEase Pro.

👁

Easy to read

Airport-sign clarity: high contrast, the Atkinson Hyperlegible typeface, large text support and reduced motion. Yellow always means one thing: an official link.
