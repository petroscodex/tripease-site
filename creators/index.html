The one rule that matters

What counts as a confirmed sale

A confirmed sale is a free user who signs up to Pro with your code or link and pays for their first Pro month. Free trials, installs and free members don't count.

Each sale is confirmed after a short hold period, so refunds are handled fairly. We confirm the length of that period when you join. Rewards you've already earned are always honoured.

Affiliate track

What does "net receipts" mean?

Affiliate rewards are a share of net receipts: what TripEase actually receives from the app stores for a customer, after store fees, sales tax and VAT, refunds and chargebacks. It isn't profit. TripEase's own running costs aren't deducted.

Affiliate track

How you get paid

  • US$100 minimum. We pay once your accrued net-receipts earnings reach US$100.
  • PayPal, Wise or Payoneer only. We collect just your payout email or Wise tag, sent in a simple email to partners@tripeaseapp.com.
  • We never ask for bank account numbers, ID or passport scans, or tax IDs.
  • Transfer fees: the payment provider's transfer fee is deducted from your payout.
  • Monthly, by hand. At first we make payouts manually, once a month.
  • Taxes: you are responsible for your own taxes on anything you earn.

Keeping it honest

Disclosure and ground rules

  • Always disclose. Say clearly that you get a reward or are sponsored, in line with US FTC and UK ASA guidance. For example: "#ad" or "I get a reward if you join with my code."
  • No misleading claims, spam or fake reviews.
  • No visa or entry guarantees. TripEase doesn't guarantee entry anywhere, and it isn't a government service.
  • Official links only. Use the links and codes we give you.

We may change the programme and will give you notice first. Either side can stop at any time.

Get started

How to join

Email us with a link to your channel and which track you'd like. We'll reply with your code and link, and confirm the remaining details.

For the Affiliate track, we check your audience first. We're looking for roughly 50,000 followers or roughly 10,000 average views on your recent posts (the average of your last 10), to be confirmed. Engagement counts for more than follower count.

  • Share the public profile link for each platform, so we can check.
  • If we're unsure, we may ask for a screenshot of your own analytics.
  • The Referrer track has no audience requirement.
