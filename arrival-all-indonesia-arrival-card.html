🇮🇩 Indonesia

All Indonesia arrival card

One free online form for immigration, customs (e-CD) and health; required for all arrivals.

Open the official site ↗

Only use the official site. Lookalike sites copy official forms and sometimes charge for them. Check the address before you type anything: it should be allindonesia.imigrasi.go.id.

At a glance

Who needs itAll foreign visitors, unless exempt.
StatusRequired
When to submitWithin 3 days before you arrive
CostFree on the official site.
Official sitehttps://allindonesia.imigrasi.go.id/
Run byDirectorate General of Immigration, Indonesia

Before you open it

Have your passport and your travel plans to hand, then answer the questions on the official site itself. We don't reproduce the form here: the official version is the only one that counts, and it can change without notice.

Get it right first time

TripEase tells you whether your passport needs All Indonesia, reminds you when it opens for your trip, and gives you one-tap access to the official form, with your details ready to copy. You always submit on the official site yourself.
