Arrival forms · 🇮🇳 India

e-Arrival Card

The Bureau of Immigration says foreign nationals and OCI card holders must complete and submit the e-Arrival Card, a free service, online within 72 hours before arriving in India, at indianvisaonline.gov.in, boi.gov.in…

Open the official site ↗

Only use the official site. Lookalike sites copy official forms and sometimes charge for them. Check the address before you type anything: it should be indianvisaonline.gov.in.

At a glance

Who needs itAll foreign visitors, unless exempt.
StatusRequired
When to submitWithin 3 days before you arrive
CostFree on the official site.
Official sitehttps://indianvisaonline.gov.in/earrival/
Run byBureau of Immigration, India

Before you open it

Have your passport and your travel plans to hand, then answer the questions on the official site itself. We don't reproduce the form here: the official version is the only one that counts, and it can change without notice.

Get it right first time

TripEase tells you whether your passport needs e-Arrival Card, reminds you when it opens for your trip, and gives you faster access to the official form, with your details ready to copy. You always submit on the official site yourself.
