Electronic Travel Authorisation (ETA)

A travel authorisation for visitors whose passport doesn't need a visa. Apply on the official site before you board your flight.

Open the official site ↗

Only use the official site. Lookalike sites copy official forms and sometimes charge for them. Check the address before you type anything: it should be www.gov.uk.

At a glance

Who needs itVisitors whose passport is covered by the scheme. Check your passport first: TripEase tells you whether you need it.
StatusRequired for covered passports
When to submitBefore you travel. See the official site for timing.
CostAn official fee is charged on the official site. Lookalike sites add their own charges on top.
Official sitehttps://www.gov.uk/eta
Run byUK Home Office

Before you open it

Have your passport and your travel plans to hand, then answer the questions on the official site itself. We don't reproduce the form here: the official version is the only one that counts, and it can change without notice.

Get it right first time

TripEase tells you whether your passport needs ETA, adds it to your trip checklist, and gives you one-tap access to the official form, with your details ready to copy. You always submit on the official site yourself.
